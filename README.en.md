## 🔍 About me  

My relationship with technology started early, tinkering with fan games, mods and ROM hacks. To balance that technical background with a human, strategic perspective, I earned a degree in Public Relations from UFSM, then spent several years in business consulting, project management and pre-sales before moving into QA. Managing crises and preventing bugs have more in common than it might seem: both depend on anticipating risks and communicating well with everyone involved.

At **Jetimob**, I grew from Testing Intern to **QA Analyst**, working on CRM, ERP, CMS, website and real estate portal products. I built my foundation in manual testing, moved on to automation with **Playwright + TypeScript**, and came to treat manual and automated testing as a single operation: BDD, Page Object Model, API testing, SQL queries in PostgreSQL and suites running in CI/CD, following a Shift-Left strategy.

I also use AI in my daily work to clear away repetitive tasks and keep the focus on what brings real value to the product:

- **Test scenarios** — I turn user stories, business rules and workflows into well-structured scenarios in a fraction of the time it would take to build them by hand.
- **Requirements review** — I review specifications to find ambiguous, contradictory or incomplete points before the test cycle begins.
- **Risk-based prioritization** — I put the scenarios with the greatest system impact first, investing effort where a failure would cost the most.
- **Traceability** — I link each scenario to the requirement it came from, keeping coverage easy to audit and true to the agreed scope.
